3 · Geography

Where bus crashes are concentrated

Bus involvement per 1,000 crashes, by LGA

LGAs with at least 25 bus-involved crashes (26 of 108) · dashed line = NSW average of 11.4
72% of bus-involved crashes are in the Sydney metropolitan area against 55% of all crashes, which mostly reflects where bus services run. Normalising by the number of crashes in each LGA changes the ranking: buses are involved in 39.9 crashes per 1,000 in North Sydney and 32.7 per 1,000 in the City of Sydney, against 11.4 statewide. The denominator here is each LGA’s own crash total.

Crash types with at least 30 crashes

What the bus collided with

Bus manoeuvre at time of crash

Rear-end collisions are the most common crash type at 343 crashes, and 15% of them are fatal or serious. Pedestrian impacts run the other way: 131 crashes, of which 63% are fatal or serious, making them the largest single source of severe outcomes. A further 74 crashes were passengers falling in or from the bus, which is a vehicle and stop-design problem rather than a road one.
5 · When

Crashes follow the timetable

Crashes by time of day (two-hour intervals)

Crashes by day of week

84% of crashes are on weekdays, peaking between 8 and 10 am and between 2 and 6 pm. 7.4% are in an active school zone. This is the shape of exposure rather than risk: those are the hours with the most services running and the most people boarding. Any comparison between routes or between times of day needs service-kilometres in the denominator.
6 · Severity

How severe these crashes are

Crash severity

Fatal and serious together = 24.8% of bus-involved crashes

% fatal or serious by speed zone

24.8% of bus-involved crashes are fatal or serious, against 22.5% for all NSW crashes. Severity rises with the speed zone, from 22% at 50 km/h to between 33% and 37% in the 80–110 km/h zones.
Prepared by Hadron Group · August 2026 · Source: Transport for NSW road crash data, published on the NSW Open Data portal · Method: releases 2016–2020 and 2020–2024 merged and deduplicated on Crash ID (183,448 crashes); bus involvement flagged where any traffic unit in the crash has TU type group = Bus (2,124 bus units in 2,092 crashes). Severe = at least one person killed or seriously injured. Crash data are police-reported, so minor incidents and on-board injuries without a collision are likely to be under-represented. 'Bus' follows the Transport for NSW definition and includes all bus types. Section 3 rates use each LGA's total crash count as the denominator; LGAs with fewer than 25 bus-involved crashes are excluded because the rate is unstable at low counts.
